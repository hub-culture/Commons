# Case studies

Cases raised in the first COP31 session. Each shows a step of the framework.

| Case | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Sharm el-Sheikh vs Cancún** | Two tourist coasts, opposite outcomes. Sharm's marine protected area keeps sea life in knee-deep water; Cancún's reefs are degraded by competing interests. The structure of the rules decides, not the amount of use. |
| **Maine lobster** | Lobstermen police their own harbour territories without formal law, the classic example of user-made rules (James Acheson, *The Lobster Gangs of Maine*, 1988). Even robust regimes leak: an egg-bearing female not returned. |
| **Mount Desert Island, Maine** | Tens of thousands of cruise visitors in summer, near-empty for six months after Labor Day, yet the forest, sea, MPA and park remain. Map a commons across all its seasons. |
| **Renewables in Spain and Portugal** | Close to a gigawatt run by one or two staff. Margins high enough to fund a citizen share. |
| **Mining and community funding** | Mining companies funding local schools: a discretionary, informal commons regime that could become a rule. |
| **The Maasai herdsman** | A herder offered cattle for a car, because a car would help him manage his herd. Nobody could have guessed the question from a distance, and value comes in local currencies. |
| **Greenland and the Arctic** | Under-covered facts: existing military agreements and freshwater. A commons too big for one country. |
| **Medicine from biodiversity** | A global benefit-sharing rule exists (the Cali Fund) but is voluntary and largely unfunded: step 2 of the ladder. |
| **Planetary protection** | Article IX of the 1967 Outer Space Treaty requires avoiding harmful contamination. Apollo 11's crew spent about three weeks in quarantine. A working global-commons rule. |
| **AI agents** | Agents interacting freely in shared networks have formed power dynamics with no rules on use: step 1 of the ladder, and the most urgent case. |
| **Tufte's maps** | The best pre-computer maps worked like calculators: change a variable and read the whole system. Gaia should treat each commons map that way. |
